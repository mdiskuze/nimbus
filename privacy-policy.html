Nimbus - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21

Nimbus is a hyper-local weather app for the Nordic countries, the Czech Republic and Germany. It has no account system, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. From version 0.51.0 it asks a small cache server of ours before it asks the weather services, which is described below. This page explains what leaves your device: where you are, and who it is sent to.

Location

If you grant Nimbus the location permission, or search for and save a place, the app sends that place's coordinates (latitude and longitude) to one or more of the weather services listed below, so they can answer with a forecast for that spot. A request that goes straight to a weather service is seen by that service and by nobody else, and a request that goes to our cache server carries only a map cell of about 2.5 km, never your coordinates (next section). There is no third party in between collecting or reselling any of it, and no location history is kept anywhere except on your own device, for your own saved places.

Our cache server

To stay within the free limits of the weather services and to answer faster, the app asks a small server of ours first for the forecast, the ensemble, the MET Norway forecast and nowcast, airport reports, SMHI's station readings and warnings, MeteoAlarm and the NOAA aurora data. It receives the size and the numbers of a map cell about 2.5 km wide around you and the names of the data the app needs, never your latitude and longitude, an account or an identifier of your phone. It stores the weather it fetched for the cells that were asked about, the list of those cells for a few days, and what weather stations measured. To limit abuse it counts the requests per hour under a short code made from the network address, the day and a secret. The counters are deleted after a few days and the code cannot be turned back into the address. We run no accounts and store no identifier of your phone. If the server does not answer, or its copy is more than half an hour old, the app asks the weather services itself, as before, and then the request carries your coordinates as described above.

Who Nimbus talks to

Every request below carries at most the coordinates needed to answer it (and, for MET Norway, an identifying app version and contact address, which is that service's own requirement for anyone using it). Some carry no coordinates at all: where a service publishes one file for a whole country, the app downloads that file and finds your place in it on your device. None of them receive a name, an account identifier, or any other data about you, because Nimbus has none to send.

What stays on your device

Saved places, cached forecasts and radar frames, and your app settings are stored locally in the app's own database, so Nimbus can show something even when it cannot reach a network. None of it is uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Closed testing builds

In the builds given to invited testers in Google Play's closed testing, and only there, the app can send a diagnostic log to the developer: which sources it called and how that went, errors, the app version, the phone model and the place as a map cell of about 2.5 km and the name of a saved place. It can also send the exact GPS position, which is off by default and which the tester has to turn on. The log is removed from the app when closed testing ends.

Notifications

Rain, wind and weather-warning alerts, and the morning brief, are calculated on your device from the same forecast data and shown as a local notification. Nothing about a notification being shown, opened or dismissed is sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Location (coarse/fine) - to fetch the forecast for where you are, if you choose to use that instead of a saved place. Optional; the app works entirely from saved places without it.
  • Notifications - for rain, wind, warning and brief alerts. Can be turned off in the app's own settings or in Android's notification settings.
  • Exact alarm / ignore battery optimisations - so the morning brief and background refresh fire on time on phones that otherwise defer them.
  • Install unknown apps - only in the version distributed outside Google Play (see below); not present in the Play Store build.

Updates, depending on where you got the app

The version installed from Google Play updates only through Google Play, the same as any other Play app. A version installed directly from GitHub instead checks that same public page for a newer release; that check reads a public GitHub URL and downloads nothing but the APK file itself.

Children

Nimbus is a general-audience weather app, not directed at children, and collects no data that would identify or track anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If what the app does changes, this page will change with it before that version is released. The date at the top is the last time it was edited.
